# Revised DL flyer and six-panel Marketing editor

## What will be built

Use the attached two-sided flyer as the design reference, retaining its wording, photograph, front/back logo placements and folding arrangement. Build a preview-only mock-up in Marketing using the Resonabed logo as the initial colour reference. Nothing will be published.

This will be a six-panel editor, rather than simply adding a third colour picker.

## Panel identification

Label each flat sheet left to right as shown in the attachment, with labels outside the printed artwork:

| Side | Panel | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Front / outside | F1 | What to expect, natural add-on and photograph |
| Front / outside | F2 | Common questions, clinic logo, contact details and booking QR |
| Front / outside | F3 | Resonabed cover logo and cover wording |
| Back / inside | B1 | What it is and the history of sound |
| Back / inside | B2 | Why people come back and Simply rest |
| Back / inside | B3 | Frequency list and wellbeing disclaimer |

The cover remains F3 in the flat-sheet view; printing and folding will retain the attached arrangement.

## How clients will use it

1. Open Marketing and see both sides of the flyer on screen, with Front and Back viewing tabs and an option to view both together.
2. Choose a panel by clicking its preview or its F1–B3 selector. The selected panel is visibly outlined outside the printed artwork.
3. Choose that panel’s background, heading/accent and body-text colours using swatches and exact hex inputs. Inset dark boxes and the cover logo backing have their own colour setting where present.
4. See changes appear automatically in the preview, including the clinic logo, contact details and QR—not just an unchanged sample image.
5. Apply a suggested logo-based palette to all panels as a starting point, then adjust individual panels. Offer coordinated dark, mid-tone and light variants rather than three unrelated colours.
6. Save the flyer design for their clinic, reset to the supplied design when needed, and download the print-ready PDF only when satisfied.

Keep logos and photographs unchanged by recolouring. Offer suitable light/dark text choices and flag poor contrast; keep the QR reliably scannable.

## Initial mock-up and review

- Show the supplied arrangement first, then a coordinated variation drawn from the owner's Resonabed logo.
- Keep all nine frequency dots visible, including 963 Hz.
- Keep clinic details aligned, allow long names to wrap and maintain clearance from folds and cut edges.
- Present the completed editor and both flyer sides in preview for review before replacing any public flyer downloads or publishing.
- Do not change the homepage, clinic website colours, pricing, payment flows or other Marketing tools.

## Technical approach

- Add a flyer-specific six-panel design model; do not overwrite the shared clinic website/theme palette.
- Store saved flyer choices separately for each organisation, with the existing access controls.
- Use explicit panel and element colour mappings for the revised artwork instead of the old blanket two-colour recolouring rule.
- Build the screen preview and PDF export from the same design settings. Render the generated PDF in the editor, with brief updating feedback, so the preview reflects the downloadable result.
- Keep the current template available while the revised mock-up is being reviewed; do not silently replace the public `/flyer` sample.
- Preserve image quality, embedded fonts, folding order, bleed and crop marks in the revised personalised export.

## Checks before completion

- Test that changing one panel does not change another and that saved designs stay isolated between clinics.
- Test valid colour settings and legacy two-colour compatibility.
- Exercise selecting panels, changing colours, saving, reopening and exporting in the signed-in Marketing view.
- Render and visually inspect both PDF pages for layout, logos, colours, all frequency dots and print clearance; verify QR destination and scanning.
- Check the editor at desktop and mobile widths, and confirm the exported PDF matches its preview.
