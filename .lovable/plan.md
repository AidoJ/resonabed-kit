# Bring the science and benefits of VAT to the front of the website

Goal: visitors learn **what VibroAcoustic Therapy is** and **why it matters** directly on the pages they land on — not by clicking through to /research. The /research page stays for depth; nothing is removed or invented.

## 1. Homepage — new "What is VAT" section (after the hero / value props, before How it works)

- Heading along the lines of "Sound you can feel. A therapy with decades of research behind it."
- Short factual explainer: what VAT is (low-frequency sound delivered through the body via tactile transducers, felt as gentle vibration while you listen), where it comes from, and that it is studied in clinical research — using only wording already verified on the /research page.
- 3–4 concise benefit cards pulled from the existing research content (relaxation/stress, perceived muscle tension, pain/comfort, sleep quality), each with the cautious "studied in VAT research" framing and the note that these studies did not test Resonabed.
- A compact version of the 3D cell animation teaser (static poster or short loop, lazy-loaded) with "Explore the science" linking to /research for the full interactive tour.
- All copy checked against the no-claims rules: no medical promises, no invented statistics, fibromyalgia context never shown as expected customer results.

## 2. How it works — extend, not replace

- Keep the three-step "How it works" as-is (that's how the *service* works for a practice).
- Add a second short block, "How VAT works in the body", briefly explaining the mechanism (vibration sensed by cells and tissues) with a link into the research page's mechanism section. Two or three sentences, plain language.

## 3. Packages / kit section — small science tie-in

- One line near the kit section tying the equipment to the therapy (e.g. the transducers are what turn the licensed frequencies into felt vibration). No changes to pricing, cards, or checkout.

## 4. Practitioner clinic pages (/o/$slug)

- Add the same short "What is VAT" explainer block to clinic pages so a clinic's own clients see the science and benefits there too, before booking.

## 5. For home page

- Add a matching brief VAT explainer so home buyers get the same grounding before the Home package.

## 6. Research page

- Unchanged as the deep-dive destination; all new sections link back to it. No duplicated long-form content on the homepage — homepage stays lightweight, animation lazy-loaded.

## Explicitly not doing

- No new claims, statistics, testimonials, or study wording beyond what is already verified on the site.
- No changes to pricing, checkout, CRM, demo page, or any private app route.
- Nothing published to production.
