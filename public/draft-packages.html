How a session runs

Four steps, every time.

1. Intake

A short guided intake on the tablet captures how the person feels today and screens for anything that should pause a session.

2. Match

The app suggests a frequency from the nine Solfeggio tracks based on the answers given.

3. Session

Headphones on, timer running, screen awake. The table carries the low frequencies through the body.

4. Close

The music fades, a soft chime ends the session, and the record is saved for next time.

Questions

Before you choose.

What is the difference between Pro and Platinum?

Only the table. Pro fits your existing treatment table, Platinum arrives as a new table with the system already fitted. The hardware, the app and the marketing material are identical.

Can I run a clinic on the Home package?

No. Home includes the personal app only. It has no bookings, no client records and no clinic webpage, and it does not include the consumables or flyers a practice needs.

Is shipping included?

No. Shipping is always calculated at checkout based on your location, and is charged on top of the package price.

Do I need my own device?

No. Every package now includes a 10 inch tablet, pre configured to run sessions and nothing else, so notifications never interrupt.
